| 2026-09-03 | [The D3D9Ex upgrade is a solved, bounded proxy problem](topics/2026-09-03-the-d3d9ex-upgrade-is-a-solved-bounded-proxy-problem.md) | 🆕 new | **⭐ Retires §9's "first thing to establish" as a blocker.** `D3DPOOL_MANAGED` really is invalid on a 9Ex device (Microsoft's own differences box), but the dichotomy "one-liner or a project" has a third answer the wrapper community settled years ago. MANAGED exists *to survive device loss* and **a 9Ex device never returns `D3DERR_DEVICELOST`** — so the pool has nothing left to do. What it does still provide is **lockability**, which is why the standard rewrite is `MANAGED → DEFAULT + D3DUSAGE_DYNAMIC`, not plain DEFAULT (the pool×usage table confirms the combination is the legal one). Shipped prior art: dxwrapper's `D3d9to9Ex`, following Special K — **7 of 8 games worked**. ⇒ the `[FLAT]` instrumented launch measures *how much Lock() traffic moves*, not whether the route exists. Real risk list replaces the single unknown: no paletted textures on 9Ex, 16-bit textures SYSTEMMEM-only, D3DX problematic, some titles fail at device creation. Also argues route (a) over `-d3d10`, since (b)'s main appeal was that (a) looked blocked. |
